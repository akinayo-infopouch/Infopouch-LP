Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Infopouch Africa collects through this website, why, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable law.

1. What we collect

When you join the waitlist, you give us your name, email address and the type of user you are (for example student, researcher or investor), plus anything else you choose to add on the form. The waitlist form is hosted by Fillout, which collects this information on our behalf.

2. Why we use it

We use this information to contact you about the Infopouch platform, including launch updates and early access, and to understand who is interested in it. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. It is shared only with service providers that help us run the website and waitlist (such as Fillout and our email provider), who may process it on our behalf, and where the law requires us to disclose it.

4. Fonts and third-party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google servers and your IP address is shared with them. The waitlist form on Fillout has its own privacy practices, which we encourage you to read.

5. How long we keep it

We keep waitlist information until the platform has launched and you have had the chance to join, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, to object to its use, or to unsubscribe from our messages at any time. Contact us at infopouchafrica@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal data, but no online service can be completely secure.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows when it last changed.
