About InfoPouch Africa

African research has always existed. What has not existed until now is one place to find it.

InfoPouch Africa is a research and professional intelligence platform built to centralise African academic output and make it accessible to the people who need it most. Students looking for local sources. Lecturers whose published work deserves to travel further. Researchers building arguments that need an African foundation. Institutions whose knowledge has been sitting in filing cabinets for too long. Professionals who need sector data that actually understands the African market.

We bring academic publications, industry datasets, and real-time sector intelligence into one searchable, organised, and growing repository so that the next generation of African researchers does not have to start from scratch, reach across the world for a reference point that does not quite fit, or submit work that is thinner than it should be simply because the right source was not where they could find it.

Our mission is straightforward. African knowledge should be findable, citable, and owned by Africa.

Our Vision

To become Africa's leading digital ecosystem for research, professional intelligence, and knowledge-driven innovation.

Our Mission

To empower students, educators, researchers, institutions, and professionals by providing accessible tools for research discovery, collaboration, analytics, and informed decision-making.

What Makes InfoPouch Africa Different

Built for African Contexts

Not a global platform with an African filter. Every feature, dataset is built around the realities of African education, industries, and African research needs.

One Place for Everything

Research discovery, academic publishing, cross-border collaboration, and professional analytics connected in a single platform so you are not switching between five tools to do one job.

Accessible by Design

Complex research and sector data presented in a way that is clean, intuitive, and usable whether you are a final year student or a senior policy professional.

Community at the Centre

Built on the belief that African knowledge compounds when African researchers can find each other, cite each other, and build on each other across disciplines and borders.

Our Core Value

Accessibility

Credibility

Verified research. Trusted data. Institutional integrity. What lives in InfoPouch Africa has earned its place there.

Innovation

We use technology to do what filing cabinets and paywalled journals could not. We make African research living, searchable, and growing.

Collaboration
